# NGNE

NGNE is a TypeScript browser engine for deterministic 2D sprite games. Its authoritative requirements are docs/architecture.md and docs/capabilities.md.

The user requests the complete engine and freely delegates the choice of a retro showcase game. Starfall '89 is an arena survival shooter demonstrating high entity churn, seeded waves, scene transitions, hitstop, input, sound and persistent progression. Platform: web. Audience: players and TypeScript game authors. Success: a playable game plus a documented, independently usable engine with measured performance. No external assets or accounts are required.

Authored choices under that delegation: keyboard, pointer, gamepad and touch controls; a three-minute survival run; an optional stress mode. No promise of performance on untested hardware.
